Florida GLP-1 & metabolic health note: All GLP-1 prescribing by Florida Mobile Health is conducted within the context of a comprehensive primary care evaluation, including full health history, physical assessment, laboratory review, and ongoing care management. Weight management and metabolic health optimization — including GLP-1 medications — are recognized primary care concerns under Florida law and fall within the autonomous FNP-BC scope of practice. Florida Mobile Health does not operate as a standalone GLP-1 prescription service. Each patient receives a complete primary care assessment before any prescription is issued.
