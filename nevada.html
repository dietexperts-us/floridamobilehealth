Preguntas comunes de pacientes en Nevada

Is Florida Mobile Health — Nevada Virtual Care licensed to practice in Nevada?

Yes. Michele Li Causi, FNP-BC holds an active advanced practice nursing license in Nevada with full autonomous prescriptive authority. She can diagnose, treat, and prescribe independently without physician oversight.

Can I get an Ozempic or Mounjaro prescription online in Nevada?

Yes, if clinically appropriate. We prescribe only the FDA-approved brand-name medications — Ozempic® (semaglutide), Mounjaro® (tirzepatide), Wegovy®, and Zepbound®. We do not prescribe compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide. Your prescription is sent directly to your preferred licensed pharmacy in Nevada.

How does a virtual visit work for Nevada residents?

You book online, complete a pre-visit health form, then meet Michele via secure video. You must be physically located in Nevada at the time of your appointment. Prescriptions go directly to your Nevada pharmacy, and lab orders can be sent to any LabCorp or Quest location near you.

Do you take insurance in Nevada?

We are a self-pay practice. No insurance is billed. We provide a detailed superbill you can submit to your insurance for possible reimbursement. HSA and FSA cards are accepted. Many patients find the cost lower than their insurance copays and deductibles.

What is the cost for a new patient visit in Nevada?

New patient visits are $149 (introductory rate, regular $200). GLP-1 consultations start at $199 intro. See our full pricing page for all services. Medication costs are separate and filled at your pharmacy.
