Understanding your A1C
Normal
Below 5.7%
Prediabetes
5.7–6.4%
Diabetes
6.5% and above

A1C reflects your average blood sugar over 3 months. Every 1% reduction in A1C reduces diabetes complications by 21%.

5 proven ways to lower blood sugar
🚶
Walk after mealsA 10–15 min walk after eating reduces glucose spikes by up to 30%
💧
Drink water firstDehydration raises blood sugar. Drink 16oz water before meals
🥗
Protein + fiber firstEat vegetables and protein before carbohydrates to slow glucose absorption
😴
Prioritize sleepOne night of poor sleep can raise fasting glucose by 20–30 mg/dL
😌
Manage stressCortisol from stress raises blood sugar. Even 5 min of deep breathing helps
📊
Use a CGMSeeing your glucose in real time is the fastest way to change your behavior
Warning signs — see a provider NOW
⚠ Excessive thirst and frequent urination
⚠ Blurry vision or vision changes
⚠ Tingling or numbness in feet/hands
⚠ Slow-healing wounds or infections
⚠ Extreme fatigue despite rest
⚠ A1C above 7% despite medication
