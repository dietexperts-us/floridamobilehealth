Understanding Blood Pressure
Know your numbers. Know your risk.

High blood pressure affects 1 in 2 adults — and most don't know they have it. Here's what your numbers actually mean and what to do about them.

6 lifestyle changes that actually lower blood pressure
🧂
Reduce sodiumCut to under 2,300mg/day. Watch processed foods — they contain 70% of dietary sodium
🚶
Move daily30 min of moderate exercise 5x/week lowers BP by 5–8 mmHg. Walking counts
🥦
DASH dietRich in potassium, magnesium, and fiber. Fruits, vegetables, whole grains, low-fat dairy
🍷
Limit alcoholNo more than 1 drink/day for women, 2 for men. Alcohol raises BP significantly
😴
Sleep 7–9 hoursSleep apnea is a major hidden cause of high blood pressure. Get screened
🧘
Manage stressChronic stress keeps BP elevated. Exercise, prayer, connection — find what works
Common myths vs facts
✗
"I feel fine so my blood pressure is fine" — hypertension has NO symptoms until it causes stroke or heart attack
✗
"I only need to take my medication when I feel bad" — BP medication must be taken daily to work
✓
Home monitoring is accurate — check at the same time daily, sitting quietly for 5 minutes first
✓
Diabetes doubles your cardiovascular risk — treating both is essential, not optional
