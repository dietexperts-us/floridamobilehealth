Understanding Menopause
Your body. Your hormones. Your health.

Menopause is not a disease — but it does change everything. Here's what's actually happening in your body and how to navigate it with clinical support.

The three phases of menopause
Perimenopause
Usually 40s–early 50s
Estrogen levels fluctuate. Periods become irregular. Hot flashes, sleep changes, and mood shifts begin. Can last 4–10 years.
Menopause
Average age: 51
12 consecutive months without a period. Estrogen levels drop significantly. Symptoms often peak during this transition.
Postmenopause
Rest of life after menopause
Estrogen stays low. Cardiovascular risk, bone loss, and metabolic changes increase. Long-term health management is essential.
Common symptoms — you are not imagining this
Hot flashes & night sweats
Sleep disruption
Mood changes
Brain fog
Weight gain (especially abdomen)
Vaginal dryness
Joint pain
Fatigue
Blood sugar changes
What actually helps
⚗️
Hormone therapy (HRT)Evidence-based, safe for most women. Dramatically reduces hot flashes, protects bones, improves mood and sleep
🥗
Protein-rich nutritionEstrogen loss accelerates muscle loss. 25–30g protein per meal preserves muscle and supports metabolism
🏋️
Strength training2–3x per week reduces bone loss, improves metabolic health, and reduces hot flash frequency
📊
CGM monitoringEstrogen drop changes insulin sensitivity. A CGM shows exactly how your metabolism is responding to hormonal changes
