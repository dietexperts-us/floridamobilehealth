Your Annual Primary Care
Checklist — What to ask for

Most people leave annual exams without getting the screenings they actually need. Here's exactly what to ask for — and why each matters.

Essential lab work every adult needs
Lab testWhat it checksHow often
Hemoglobin A1C3-month blood sugar average — catches diabetes earlyAnnually (more if prediabetic)
Fasting lipid panelCholesterol, triglycerides, cardiovascular riskEvery 4–6 years (annually if high risk)
Complete metabolic panelKidney function, liver function, electrolytesAnnually
TSH (thyroid)Thyroid function — affects weight, energy, moodEvery 2–3 years (annually for women 50+)
CBC (blood count)Anemia, infection, immune healthAnnually
Vitamin DDeficiency linked to diabetes, depression, bone lossAnnually (especially in FL heat)
Blood pressureCardiovascular risk — "silent killer"Every visit
Preventive screenings by age
Diabetes screening
Every 3 years starting at 35 (or earlier if overweight)
Nearly 1 in 3 adults over 45 has prediabetes. Caught early, it is reversible.
Blood pressure
Every healthcare visit, every adult
Half of adults with hypertension don't know they have it. No symptoms until crisis.
Colorectal cancer
Starting at age 45, every 10 years
3rd most common cancer. Colonoscopy catches polyps before they become cancer.
Mammogram (women)
Annually starting at 40–45
Early detection reduces breast cancer mortality by 40%.
Pap smear (women)
Every 3 years ages 21–65
Detects cervical cancer and HPV. Highly preventable when caught early.
Bone density (DEXA)
Women 65+, or postmenopausal with risk factors
Osteoporosis affects 1 in 2 women over 50. Estrogen loss accelerates bone loss.
5 questions to ask your provider every year
1
"What are all my lab results?"Don't accept "everything looks fine" — ask for actual numbers and what they mean
2
"Am I due for any screenings?"Use this checklist — providers often skip screenings unless prompted
3
"Is my weight affecting my health?"Frame it medically — metabolic health, not appearance
4
"Are all my vaccinations current?"Flu, COVID, shingles (50+), pneumonia, tetanus boosters
