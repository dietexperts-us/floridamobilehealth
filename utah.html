Frequently asked questions

Common questions from Utah patients

Is Florida Mobile Health — Utah Virtual Care licensed to practice in Utah?

Yes. Michele Li Causi, FNP-BC holds an active advanced practice nursing license in Utah with full autonomous prescriptive authority. She can diagnose, treat, and prescribe independently without physician oversight.

Can I get an Ozempic or Mounjaro prescription online in Utah?

Yes, if clinically appropriate. We prescribe only the FDA-approved brand-name medications — Ozempic® (semaglutide), Mounjaro® (tirzepatide), Wegovy®, and Zepbound®. We do not prescribe compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide. Your prescription is sent directly to your preferred licensed pharmacy in Utah.

How does a virtual visit work for Utah residents?

You book online, complete a pre-visit health form, then meet Michele via secure video. You must be physically located in Utah at the time of your appointment. Prescriptions go directly to your Utah pharmacy, and lab orders can be sent to any LabCorp or Quest location near you.

Do you take insurance in Utah?

We are a self-pay practice. No insurance is billed. We provide a detailed superbill you can submit to your insurance for possible reimbursement. HSA and FSA cards are accepted. Many patients find the cost lower than their insurance copays and deductibles.

What is the cost for a new patient visit in Utah?

New patient visits are $149 (introductory rate, regular $200). GLP-1 consultations start at $199 intro. See our full pricing page for all services. Medication costs are separate and filled at your pharmacy.
