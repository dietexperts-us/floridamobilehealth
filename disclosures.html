Scope of Practice Alcance de práctica

All services are provided by Michele Li Causi, FNP-BC, RD, CDCES within her licensed scope of practice as a board-certified Family Nurse Practitioner, Registered Dietitian, and Certified Diabetes Care and Education Specialist.

Services include evaluation, diagnosis, treatment, ordering and interpreting diagnostic tests, and prescribing medications within the FNP specialty in each licensed state.

Los servicios incluyen evaluación, diagnóstico, tratamiento, pedido e interpretación de pruebas diagnósticas y prescripción de medicamentos dentro de la especialidad FNP en cada estado con licencia.

Controlled Substances Sustancias controladas

Florida Mobile Health does not prescribe controlled substances, including Schedule II–V medications such as opioids, benzodiazepines, stimulants, sedative-hypnotic sleep medications, or testosterone.

Patients requiring testosterone therapy or other controlled substances will be referred to an appropriate provider.

GLP-1 medications (Ozempic, Mounjaro, Wegovy), estrogen, and progesterone are not classified as controlled substances and are prescribed within our scope of practice.

Florida Mobile Health no prescribe sustancias controladas, incluyendo medicamentos de los Schedule II–V como opioides, benzodiacepinas, estimulantes o medicamentos para dormir.

Los pacientes que requieran sustancias controladas serán referidos a un proveedor apropiado. Los medicamentos GLP-1 (Ozempic, Mounjaro, Wegovy) y las terapias hormonales no están clasificados como sustancias controladas y se prescriben dentro de nuestro alcance de práctica.

Mental Health Services Servicios de salud mental

Mental health support, pharmacogenomic testing, and antidepressant medication management are provided within the FNP scope of practice.

Florida Mobile Health does not provide formal psychiatric diagnoses or initiate psychiatric medications for conditions outside the FNP scope of practice. Patients requiring psychiatric diagnosis, complex psychiatric medication management, or specialized mental health treatment will be referred to a licensed psychiatrist or mental health professional.

Pharmacogenomic testing is used to support medication selection and does not constitute a psychiatric diagnosis.

El apoyo en salud mental, las pruebas farmacogenómicas y el manejo de medicamentos antidepresivos se proporcionan dentro del alcance de práctica de la FNP.

Florida Mobile Health no proporciona diagnósticos psiquiátricos formales ni inicia medicamentos psiquiátricos para condiciones fuera del alcance de práctica de la FNP. Los pacientes que requieran diagnóstico psiquiátrico serán referidos a un psiquiatra o profesional de salud mental.

Las pruebas farmacogenómicas se utilizan para apoyar la selección de medicamentos y no constituyen un diagnóstico psiquiátrico.
